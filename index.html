Muhammad Huzaifah is an international architect and urban researcher based in Berlin, Germany. As a prestigious DAAD Scholar at Brandenburgische Technische Universität Cottbus-Senftenberg (BTU Cottbus), his academic research and professional practice investigate heritage regeneration, ecological revitalisation, and sustainable urban transformation.

As the founding director of ScapeCraft, Huzaifah orchestrates multidisciplinary project delivery spanning the United States, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. His methodology blends rigorous architectural logic, parametric BIM documentation (LOD 300–350), and cinematographic 3D visualizations to transform complex spatial ideas into constructible, culturally grounded architecture.

Studio DNA & Philosophy

Form Exploration, Material Honesty & Sustainable Logic

ScapeCraft was founded in 2017 with an unwavering conviction: architecture is not merely physical construction, but the delicate calibration of space, light, materiality, and ecological responsibility.

We avoid repetitive aesthetic formulas. Every proposal—whether an intimate mountain retreat in Northern Pakistan, a commercial tower in Manhattan, or an arid urban promenade in Dubai—is born from hyper-local contextual inquiry, solar and microclimate analysis, and collaborative dialogue with our clients.

Carbon Conscience
